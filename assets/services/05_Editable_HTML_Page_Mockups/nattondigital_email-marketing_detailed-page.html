What this service does

Understand Email Marketing & Automation

Email marketing allows brands to educate subscribers, share updates and help customers take the next step. Successful programmes focus on permission, relevance and useful information rather than sending high volumes of unsolicited messages.

An engagement may include list segmentation, templates, campaign scheduling, welcome sequences and deliverability monitoring, with opt-out and privacy controls.
