What this service does

Understand Google Business Profile (GMB/GBP)

Google My Business (GMB) is now called Google Business Profile (GBP). An eligible, well-maintained Business Profile can help customers find your business on Google Search and Maps, see opening hours, check services and choose how to contact you.

Profile management should begin by checking eligibility and ownership, then verifying details, selecting the most accurate categories and keeping photos, service information and hours current. Review responses, updates and performance reporting should follow Google’s policies; no one can guarantee map-pack rankings or verification approval.

Frequently asked questions

Is GMB the same as Google Business Profile? +

Yes. Google My Business is the older name; Google Business Profile is the current product name.

Can every online business create a profile? +

No. Google has eligibility requirements, including in-person customer contact for many categories.

Can you promise top-three Google Maps ranking? +

No. Visibility depends on several factors outside any agency’s control.
