Service introduction

What is WhatsApp Automation?

WhatsApp automation can make business conversations more organised by routing enquiries, sending approved template messages and updating CRM records. It can support bookings, reminders and customer support when customers have opted in and channel policies are followed.

A good service page explains the difference between basic WhatsApp Business features and the official Business Platform API. It should also explain template approval, costs, opt-outs and human support.

Why choose this solution

Practical benefits for your business

This service is designed for businesses using whatsapp as a primary customer communication channel.

  • Timely replies: Respond to inbound questions without losing the conversation.
  • Organised reminders: Send approved booking or service updates when allowed.
  • One customer history: Give authorised staff useful context during handoff.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about WhatsApp Automation

Can we send messages to any phone number? +

No. Follow WhatsApp policies and applicable law, including valid opt-in requirements.

Are message charges included? +

Pricing depends on Meta and service-provider rates and should be shown separately.

Can customers speak to a person? +

Yes, a staff handoff should be part of the recommended flow.
